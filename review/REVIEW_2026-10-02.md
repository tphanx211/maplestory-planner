## Class pick

### Blind result (Track 6)
Before reading the plan, the blind track scored all ten 2nd jobs with one model at steady state, about Lv 50 on Wraith. It ranked both **farmers** and **launch characters**.

**Farmer: Fighter (2H axe).** Net mesos/h:
- Fighter 118k
- F/P 103k
- Page 90k
- I/L 84k (#7)

By Oct 21: Fighter ~5.9M, I/L ~3.1M.

I/L has the 2nd-highest **gross** kill rate, but MP eats about half of its ~96 mesos per Wraith (AoE damage per MP: I/L 155.6 vs Fighter 556.3).

**What flips it to I/L:**
- Thunder Bolt averaging 4+ targets at 65%+ uptime, **or**
- MP at ≤ 0.5 mesos/MP.

**Launch character: Assassin** (Cleric as the support alternative).

### Devil's advocate (Track 7), on your four arguments

| # | Argument | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Physical accuracy bottleneck; mages never miss | **Fails.** Same hit check for spells. Mage and Fighter ACC curves stay within 2–4 points of each other at every level. Wands need LUK, just as axes need DEX |
| 2 | Vendor equipment (15–45k) and scroll drops | **Partly.** More kills means more drop rolls. But typical sell prices are 3–9k, 45k doesn't exist, and scrolls sell to NPCs for 1 meso. The extra income is class-neutral per kill |
| 3 | Vertical maps avoid congestion | **Fails.** Thunder Bolt is ground-only, about one platform tall, and its best maps are the contested Ant Tunnels and subway lines |
| 4 | Lv 48–65 post-launch window | **Holds in meowdb's model.** I/L gets ~1.8–2.2× the Fighter's EXP/min at Lv 50–70, if every cast hits all 6 mobs **and** the cap is ≥ 60 |

### My judgement
- **For mesos by Oct 21, the Fighter is better.**
  - In the review's model on the same corrected inputs (mid case): Fighter ≈ Lv 57 / 6.7M coin mesos; I/L ≈ Lv 51 / 3.9M.
  - Equipment drops add to both equally.
- **Your weighting makes I/L defensible.** You put hundreds of post-launch hours at Lv 48–65 first, and I/L's modeled advantage there is real.
- **It only works if** Thunder Bolt hits 2+ (ideally 4+) mobs per cast on uncontested maps, MP stays at 1 meso/MP or less, and the cap is ≥ 60.
- **The v0.6.0 route is built around those conditions:**
  - Thunder Bolt first, with MP Eater 1 at Lv 30.
  - Low-congestion, low-magic-DEF maps.
  - An accuracy table that sets map levels.
- **Three day-1 checks tell you within a week whether the bet is paying:**
  - **V-38:** misses.
  - **V-39:** equipment drops per 1,000 kills.
  - **V-40:** Thunder Bolt targets per cast.
- **Assassin stands as the launch character, with the tripwire.**

- **Drop rates of any kind** (equipment, scrolls, stars): not published or datamined. meowdb has votes only, and the MSEA lists carry no rates. Sites quoting rates (henesys.gg, maplestoryclassic.wiki) were unsourced or SEO-grade.

- **Launch values:** everything is from the COT2 test build (meowdb snapshot 2026-08-04). Launch numbers may differ.
- **Level cap, world count, character slots at launch, Hired Merchant availability, Cash Shop prices:** nothing official yet.
- **Whether two Classic clients or multiple accounts are allowed:** no Global statement. One login per account is legacy behaviour.
- **The accuracy formula:** it comes from meowdb's tools, not a stated datamine [model]. V-38 checks it in game.
- **Real-play kill rates and Thunder Bolt targets per cast:** the review's I/L model is 2.5–5× below meowdb's Advisor, so absolute hours and mesos are uncertain. V-36 and V-40 measure them.
- **Lv 50+ EXP:** legacy table. A level-gap EXP penalty is unknown (V-41).
- **Whether the desktop app honours a project `defaultMode` for scheduled runs:** I couldn't test it without running a task, which the review rules forbid.
- **Verifiers were grouped by theme.** Each finding was still tested individually, but a single verifier saw several related findings.
