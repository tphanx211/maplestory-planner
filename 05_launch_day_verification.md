## Part A: First 2 hours on Oct 6 (quick card)

### Before Oct 6
- [ ] **Client.** The Nexon Launcher's Classic client can **only be downloaded on launch day** (a "MapleStory Classic World" toggle appears). Steam players: install and update base MapleStory (app 216150) **now** and link your Nexon account [V-off N2].
- [ ] **Clear your Oct 6 afternoon.** Servers open at 1:00 PM CDT. Expect a queue.
- [ ] Have this card and a notepad (or the dashboard's price log) open on the second monitor.
- [ ] **Decide your IGNs.** Founders get first pick; IGNs are separate from regular MapleStory [V-off N1]. Have 2–3 backups for the I/L Wizard and the Assassin.

### Hour 0: login and creation (1:00–1:15 PM CDT)
- [ ] **World list.** Record how many worlds exist and their names (V-3). If there's more than one, **pick the world your friends can join on Oct 21**. If unsure, pick the one Nexon recommends to new players.
- [ ] **Character slots.** On the character select screen, count the empty slots (V-2).
- [ ] **Create the I/L farmer** (Magician). Starting stats: **4 STR / 4 DEX / 13 INT / 4 LUK** (every stat starts at 4; put all 9 free points in INT). After that, all AP in INT, plus LUK only up to your next wand's requirement (10 by Lv 20, 20 by Lv 30) (`01` §7).
- [ ] Pick a **low-population channel**. Avoid Channel 1, which hosts KPQ [V-cbt M-KPQ].

### Hour 0–1: Maple Island and the first measurements
- [ ] Play Maple Island normally (route §1–10).
- [ ] **Meso tally #1 (V-1, most important).** Pick one mob type, note your mesos, kill 50, and note your mesos again. Record: mob, kills, mesos gained, and how many kills dropped mesos. Use the template in Part C.
- [ ] Check the **inventory** window: slots per tab (Equip, Use, Set-up, Etc) (V-6).
- [ ] Open the **Cash Shop** and record the prices of the items in V-7. **Don't buy yet.** Buy rules are in `00` §3.6.

### Hour 1–2: Victoria Island
- [ ] **Leave Maple Island**, then open the **mailbox**, claim the Zakum package and **open it right away**: once claimed you have **30 days to open it**. Package items can't be opened on Maple Island; claim by Nov 30, 23:59 UTC [V-off N10, CMS 44134].
- [ ] **Storage**: at the first storage NPC, record starting slots, the deposit fee for an item, the withdrawal fee, and whether depositing mesos costs anything (V-4, V-5).
- [ ] **NPC potion prices**: spot-check Red 50, **Orange 50 (50 MP)**, Lemon 150 and Blue 220 at the grocer (V-12). Buy MP only as Orange or Lemon.
- [ ] **1st job at Lv 10: Magician**, with Grendel the Really Old in **Ellinia**; there's no INT requirement [V-dm M-Q1017]. First SP into Energy Bolt (`01` §8).
- [ ] **Meso tally #2** on a Lv 10–20 mob (V-1).
- [ ] **At Lv 12: sign up for Kerning citizenship** with Roxy. Kerning sells the Overall INT Lesser (Ben, grade 7) and Wolbi for the Assassin (Max, grade 3), and the first town choice is free [V-dm M-CIT]. Do the first daily right away. Read the board for the daily task, the weekly donation list, and whether contribution resets on promotion (V-9).

**Top 5 to check in your first hour:**
1. Meso tally on one mob type (V-1).
2. World count and name (V-3).
3. Character slots (V-2).
4. Cash Shop prices for the pet and pet skills, Store Permit and Hired Merchant (V-7).
5. Inventory slots per tab (V-6).

Storage (V-4) comes right after you leave Maple Island.
