# Dual-Character Operations (from Oct 21)

Version 0.6 · 2026-10-02. Tags and source keys are as in `00_classic_world_verified_intel.md`.

Two characters (your decision, 0.6):
- **I/L Wizard**: farmer, bank and merchant. **Kerning citizen** (Overall INT Lesser from Ben at grade 7) [V-dm M-CIT].
- **Assassin**: launch character, levelled with friends. **Kerning citizen** (Wolbi at grade 3).

Both in Kerning means one storage discount (Mr. Hong) serves both. If citizenship turns out to be per account (V-32), that's the town you already share.

**Baseline: one client at a time.** Running MapleStory and Classic together is not allowed, and two Classic clients aren't addressed anywhere [V-off N1; ? V-16]. Both characters are on **one account**, and one account can only have one character logged in [B legacy behaviour]. So the farmer can't stand in a shop while you play the Assassin.

## 2. Moving mesos and items between your characters

### 2.1 Confirmed rules
- **Shared account storage** holds items and mesos for every character on the account [V-dm M-NPC].
  - Storage NPCs: Mr. Lee (Henesys Park), Mr. Hong (Kerning), Mr. Kim (Lith), Mr. Park (Ellinia), Mr. Wang (Perion), Mr. Oh (Sleepywood), Scrooge (FM Entrance), Cave Fairy (Forgotten Hollow).
- **Deposit fee: 100 mesos per item** [V-dm].
  - A citizenship grade cuts it 5% per grade (down to 50 at grade 10), but **only at your own town's storage**: Kerning, Mr. Hong, for both characters.
  - Unknown [?] (V-5): the withdrawal fee, the meso deposit/withdraw fee, and whether a stack counts as one item.
- **Cash and cosmetic items** move through the **Cash Inventory**, same account and same world only [V-off N10].
- **With one client you can't trade your own characters face to face.** Storage is the only route.

### 2.2 Transfer run (about 3 min)
1. On the I/L, go to **Kerning storage (Mr. Hong)** for the citizenship discount.
2. Deposit the items in order: **stars and scrolls the Assassin needs this week** → **hold-inventory overflow** → **quest ETC stock**.
3. Stack items before depositing.
4. **Deposit mesos** for the Assassin's weekly allowance (§4).
5. Log out, switch to the Assassin, and withdraw at Mr. Hong.

Do **one transfer run per play day at most**. Batching keeps fees and switching time down.

### 2.3 Storage slot budget (assumes 50 slots until V-4 says otherwise)

| Use | Slots | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Assassin funding queue (stars, scrolls, next claw) | 12 | Refill from the I/L weekly |
| Hold-thesis inventory (Ilbi/Steely/Tobi, GFA/GFMA, Overall stat, Earring/Cape stat, Chaos) | 20 | `02` §3 |
| Quest-ETC stock for sale (Mushmom Spore, Medicine, Skulls, Skins, Rubble, Necki Skins, Dolls) | 10 | Sell in stacks (`02` §6) |
| Buffer | 8 | Drops waiting for a decision |

Buy the **Storage Room 5-slot Coupon** (Cash Shop) when the buffer stays full for 3+ days. "Whatever pays off" applies: each coupon must hold items worth more than its price.

## 3. Bank rules (I/L)
- The I/L holds the account's meso stock and its hold inventory. It follows the buckets in `02` §1.4: ≥40% progression reserve, ≤35% holds, ≤25% flips, ≤10% in any one item.
- The **progression reserve covers both characters**, including **both potion bills**: the I/L's 18–40k/h and the Assassin's ~29k/h (§4).
- **Internal supply first, where the I/L can supply it:**
  - **Yes:** Mokbi (Jr. Wraith), Kumbi (Lupin), Wolbi (Fire Boar), Claw ATT Intermediate (Zombie Mushroom), Steely (Red Drake, ice weak), GFA60 from the I/L's own Cursed Dolls / Jane rolls, and Ilbi from Mushmom if you take the Cold Beam boss variant (`01` §8).
  - **No:** Claw ATT Greater (class-gated quest reward; use the Assassin's own copies), Hwabi (no confirmed source), Earring Crit Damage Lesser (Henesys-only NPC; buy from players). Market-buy those at R2.

## 4. Funding the Assassin [calc; prices are NPC values, V-dm]

| Assassin level | Needs | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 10–30 | Subi (500/set), Garnier → Steel Titans (5,000 / 7,000), Orange/Lemon | NPC; the Assassin can pay its own way |
| 22+ | **Wolbi** (1,000/set at Kerning grade 3) | NPC (Kerning citizen) |
| 20–30 | Steel Igor (14,100) or Meba (16,500), then **Adamantium Guards** (27,600) at 30 | NPC (Cutthroat Manny) |
| 30–35 | Mokbi/Kumbi | I/L stash |
| 35–50 | Dark Guardian → Dark Slain (craft or market), **Claw ATT Lesser ×7 = 350,000**, Work Gloves + GFA, Tobi/Ilbi | El Moth; the Assassin's own quest Greaters; I/L drops; market at R2 |
| Ongoing | **MP potions: ~29k–48k mesos per hour** of Lucky Seven play (16 MP × ~1,800–3,000 casts/h at 1 meso/MP) [calc] | The I/L's farming |

- **Rule of thumb** [calc, `01` §0]: the I/L nets about **40–60k mesos/h at Lv 30–38, 75–100k at Lv 41–50, 110–130k at Lv 51–60** (mid; low is about half). So **1 I/L hour funds about 1–4 Assassin hours** of potion upkeep before gear: 40k ÷ 48k ≈ 0.8 at worst, 120k ÷ 29k ≈ 4 at best (mid ≈ 2).
- **Weekly allowance:** the Assassin's potion cost for its expected weekly hours (§5) plus the next gear milestone in the table. Deposit it in one transfer run.

## 5. Daily schedule (from Oct 21; one character at a time)

Your friends' play times are unknown, so these are templates. Put the **"with friends"** block wherever they play, and the I/L blocks around it. **Limit yourself to 2–3 character switches per session**; each logout and login costs a minute or two [?].

### Weekday (4 h)
| Block | Character | What |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | I/L | Citizenship daily (and VIP daily from grade 5) · boss check (Mushmom channel and time log, if you run the boss variant) · log watchlist prices · **transfer run** (§2.2) |
| 0:20–3:20 | Assassin | Citizenship daily · level with friends. When they're offline, solo on the class map (`01` §9) |
| 3:20–4:00 | I/L | Farm block, **or** a 20–30 min Store Permit session if you have stock to clear (`02` §2) |

### Weekend day (8 h)
| Block | Character | What |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–2:00 | I/L | Farm block (best map for its level, `01` §4–6) + boss loop |
| 2:00–2:30 | I/L | Shop session or listing round; weekly citizenship donation; **transfer run** |
| 2:30–7:30 | Assassin | With friends; weekly donation; KPQ if the friend group has 4 players and V-19 showed it's worth it |
| 7:30–8:00 | I/L | Restock, log prices, export the dashboard data |

This gives the I/L about 1 h per weekday and 3 h per weekend day (~11 h a week). If your post-launch goal is mainly farming, move hours from the Assassin block, not from the transfer run.

### Overnight
- **If the Hired Merchant is sold (V-8):** before logging off, place it with the I/L in an FM room, stocked with `02` R3 listings. It runs 24 h; collect at Fredrick the next day. **This is the farmer's main merchant role.**
- **If it isn't sold:** nothing runs overnight. Don't use any automation; it's bannable [V-off N9].

### Two clients or a second account (gray area; don't do it without a written OK)
- Selling on the I/L **while** playing the Assassin would need a **second account** and a **second client**. Nexon hasn't addressed either for Classic (V-16, V-29). Account sharing is banned [V-off N9].
- Don't do this on a guess. Get a written answer from Nexon support or the FAQ first.
